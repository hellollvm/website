The Chengdu meetup is the 4th stop of the "Ten Cities Meetup" tour organized by the HelloGCC, HelloLLVM and CNRV communities. As a city with a very active crowd of innovators, we hope we can find a venue big enough for everyone.

Starting in February 2023, we will spend 10 months holding offline tech exchange meetups in 10 different cities. This series is jointly planned by the HelloGCC community, the HelloLLVM community and CNRV (RISC-V China community). After a long separation, please make this reunion gloriously over the top. In every city you can expect to meet interesting peers — it's going to be lively.

## Format

A relaxed offline meetup. There is a projector on site, and there may be a few informal tech talks. Snacks and fruit will be provided so everyone can chat while eating and drinking.

## You can still sign up to give a talk

- Talk content can be about the patches you are working on lately, development insights, community gossip, and so on. Anything related to toolchains, emulators, virtual machines, kernels, RISC-V etc. is welcome. Introductions to progress in new technology areas are welcome too.
- There is no strict limit on talk length: it can be a 5-minute lightning talk or a 25-minute long talk. Tutorials on new languages and new tools are welcome.
