Finally, we can confidently meet offline again.

Starting in February 2023, HelloLLVM will spend 10 months holding offline tech exchange meetups in 10 different cities. This series is jointly planned by the HelloGCC community, the HelloLLVM community and the CNRV community (China's RISC-V developer community). After a long separation, please make this reunion gloriously over the top. In every city you can expect to meet interesting peers — it's going to be lively.

As organizers, we chose Wuhan as the starting point of the series — with gratitude, respect, and a certain sense of ritual. Let us start over again, singing as we go.

## Time and Venue

Saturday, February 25, 2023, 15:00 – 18:00

Hubei High-Tech Innovation Workshop (湖北高投双创工坊), No. 2 Muxiang Road, Guandong Street, Hongshan District, Wuhan, Hubei (exact room TBA — watch for follow-up notices)

Online participation details TBA.

## Format

A relaxed offline meetup. There is a projector on site, and there may be a few informal tech talks. Snacks and fruit will be provided so everyone can chat while eating and drinking.

## Tech talk content and format

- Talk content can be about the patches you are working on lately, development insights, community gossip, and so on. Anything related to toolchains, emulators, virtual machines, kernels, RISC-V etc. is welcome. Introductions to progress in new technology areas are welcome too.
- There is no strict limit on talk length: it can be a 5-minute lightning talk or a 25-minute long talk. Tutorials on new languages and new tools are welcome.
