Shenzhen is the third stop of the "Ten Cities Meetup" tour jointly organized by HelloGCC, HelloLLVM, CNRV and friends. As a city with a very active crowd of innovators, we hope we can find a venue big enough for everyone.

Starting in February 2023, we will spend 10 months holding offline tech exchange meetups in 10 different cities. This series is jointly planned by the HelloGCC community, the HelloLLVM community and the CNRV community (China's RISC-V developer community). After a long separation, please make this reunion gloriously over the top. In every city you can expect to meet interesting peers — it's going to be lively.

## Time and Venue

Saturday, April 22, 2023, 14:30 – 17:30

Address: A301, Industry-Academia Base, Shenzhen Virtual University Park, No. 2 Yuexing 3rd Road, Nanshan District, Shenzhen (Roadshow Hall, Lanzhou University Shenzhen Research Institute)

## Agenda (constantly evolving)

Since many friends signed up to share, we may need to dynamically negotiate the time of each talk. Please sit back, relax, and keep maximum flexibility about the uncertainty of the agenda timing.

- Hua Lin — A casual talk on AI chip architectures
- Zhang Yu — Introduction to the Moonbit language
- Wu Caize — Introduction to LicheePi 4A, a high-performance RISC-V SBC based on TH1520
- Peng Weilin — Introduction to ArtInChip's RISC-V industrial SoC series
- Jia Xianhua — Real-time preemption on RISC-V D1
- Niu Fukun — Static call support for RISC-V
- Wu Zhangjin — TinyLinux porting for RISC-V
- Wu Zhangjin — RISC-V Linux kernel research campaign: report and demo (bring a laptop to follow along with the Tinylab experiment disks)
- Talks registered on site or registered but forgotten (if any)
