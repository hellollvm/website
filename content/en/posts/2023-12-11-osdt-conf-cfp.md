The 15th Open Source Development Tools Conference (formerly HelloGCC Workshop) is planned for January 13–14, 2024, with a main venue in Beijing plus online attendance. The conference is the annual developer gathering organized by the OSDT community (formerly the HelloGCC community), where we share our open source development work, research results and lessons learned. Topics mainly target open source development tools, including GCC, Binutils, GDB, glibc, Clang/LLVM, QEMU, gem5, Chisel/FIRRTL, MLIR, TVM, CIRCT and more.
